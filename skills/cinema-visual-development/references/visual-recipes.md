# Adaptable visual recipes

These are authored starting points, not recovered settings or locally validated presets.

| Intent | Staging and camera | Light/sound | Failure to inspect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emotional realization | Held medium close-up; eye movement before body turn; cut only after recognition | Motivated soft side source; let a sound prompt the glance | Face morphing, unmotivated smile, premature cut |
| World reveal | Foreground establishes scale; controlled move reveals consequential information | Maintain light direction through reveal; distant ambience becomes specific | Geometry swimming, scale change, empty spectacle |
| Discovery | Establish what is being sought; eyeline then object or delayed reveal | Sound may lead picture; preserve object position | Reverse eyeline, impossible object placement |
| Product material | Owned/approved geometry or plate; controlled reflection and modest move | Broad source for readable surface; detail-specific Foley | Logo/text drift, material changing, exaggerated claim |
| Contact/action | Clear approach, contact, reaction and release; simplify camera if contact is difficult | Sound transient matches visible contact | Floating feet, penetration, broken momentum |
| Final image | Revisit an earlier composition with a changed relationship | Return or transform a sound motif | New spectacle without emotional payoff |

Continuity table columns: scene/shot; entering state; outgoing state; reference IDs; screen direction; object hand/position; light source; wardrobe; performance; sound bridge; accepted exception.

Lighting families are not emotional laws. Soft light can be threatening; daylight can be tragic. Choose the relationship to the scene. For a lighting recipe record key position, source size relative to subject, fill intention, practicals, separation and what should remain unlit.

Generation brief example: One repairer sits at the left of a wooden table, holding a small recorder in the right hand. The thumb approaches the erase button, stops, then withdraws. Camera holds a medium close-up from the same side of the table. Soft window light comes from frame right. Preserve the face and jacket from the appearance reference and the hand/table arrangement from the blocking reference. This is a proposed test; small controls and readable lettering may require compositing.
