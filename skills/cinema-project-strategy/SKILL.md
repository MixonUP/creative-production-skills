# Choose a film worth producing

Read the current brief and available project evidence. In this workspace start with `E:/AI_films/Knowledge/00_HOME.md`; use only relevant cards. A user's preferred theme or artistic ambition is a constraint to develop, not a low score to optimize away.

## Define the decision

Identify the audience, intended feeling, format/duration, distribution, buyer or revenue mechanism, schedule, cash ceiling and available people/assets. Reuse established answers. If something consequential is unknown, ask a small strategic question while continuing independent research. Mark provisional assumptions rather than inventing market data.

For a commercial choice, distinguish commissioned revenue, portfolio value, owned-IP development and platform revenue. A festival award, view count or impressive film does not establish profitability. Record the source/date of demand signals, client prices and platform rules. Do not extrapolate a viral outlier into normal results.

## Compare options without flattening taste

For this studio's repeatable analytics and handoff into writing, read [story-intelligence.md](references/story-intelligence.md). It routes to the local evidence schema and tested CLI; it does not supply market data or establish a winning model by itself.

Use [decision-model.md](references/decision-model.md) when comparing alternatives. Keep artistic conviction, audience evidence and production risk separately visible. Show a small set of meaningfully different options, their strongest objection and the cheapest experiment that could change the choice. Do not generate dozens of cosmetic variations just to fill a matrix.

Choose a recommendation and a credible fallback. Explain whether the choice remains the same under pessimistic cost/demand assumptions. When data is missing, propose validation rather than fake precision. Preserve an author-led candidate even if it lacks immediate sales evidence when that serves the brief.

## Deliver

Save a decision memo: brief, options, evidence, assumptions, cost range, artistic rationale, selected direction, kill/revisit conditions and next deliverable. Hand the selected premise to `cinema-development` when script development is requested. Research or planning does not itself start spending, outreach or publication.
