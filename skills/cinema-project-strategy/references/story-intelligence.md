Entry point: E:/AI_films/Knowledge/Story_Intelligence/START_HERE.md.
Method: E:/AI_films/Knowledge/Story_Intelligence/METHOD_RU.md.
Tool: E:/AI_films/Tools/StoryIntelligence/studio.py (Python standard library).

Use templates/portfolio.json as a starting contract, not as assessed data. Keep sources, dates, claims and limitations; observations, source claims, assumptions and editorial judgments remain distinct. Collect actual relevant sources through available research tools before assigning audience/buyer support. Do not treat repository demonstration costs or model-generated audience reactions as market evidence.

`validate INPUT` checks structure. `analyze INPUT --output NEW_DIR` gives separate art/audience/production intervals, missing data, cost scenarios and weight sensitivity. Read both the input snapshot and report: coverage means ratings were entered, not that they are verified. There is no automatic overall winner. Author-protected ideas remain available; constraints and economics are considered separately from the limited Pareto signal.

After an explicit or provisional development choice, `packet INPUT --idea ID --output NEW_DIR` creates writer/editor tasks and a draft production contract. Mark provisional choices. Use cinema-development to execute the writing task; creating a prompt file is not writing a screenplay. Generated outputs use a new directory and never replace a prior decision record.

At a later production/audience test, update a new input version with observations, actual accepted output, time, expenditure and feedback. Record why the decision changed. The CLI has no crawler, external model calls, publishing or scheduled monitoring.

For current audience/style research, use E:/AI_films/Knowledge/Trend_Intelligence/START_HERE.md and Tools/TrendRadar/radar.py. Public RSS discovery, platform claims, forecasts, comparable measurements and personal taste are different evidence types. Imported source counts are not popularity; a repeated publisher is not independent corroboration. Export evidence into a new portfolio version without automatically assigning idea scores.

For paid-project selection and business models, read E:/AI_films/Knowledge/StudioBusiness/PLAYBOOK_RU.md. Tools/StudioBusiness/quote.py computes explicit cost/revision scenarios including owner labor and sales effort. Repository prices are proposed examples, not proven market rates or revenue forecasts. Distinguish the surplus after modeled labor/deductions from accounting net profit. Verify the user's applicable distribution/payment situation before relying on platform revenue; do not infer location or eligibility merely from timezone.
