# Choose a route on evidence

Define the target shot family and failure first. Read the dated provider/model/endpoint/mode adapter. Verify current primary documentation for actual capabilities and prices before constructing a paid run. Keep documentation claims separate from measured local support. An existing cheap route remains a baseline until another route demonstrates a relevant advantage.

## Design a useful comparison

Use [evaluation-protocol.md](references/evaluation-protocol.md). Compare the same creative brief, equivalent references and a stated time/cost envelope. Keep endpoint-required prompt differences in the record. For a causal test change one factor; when comparing complete production routes, disclose all differences and do not attribute the result to one parameter.

Start with a small falsification test of the hardest needed capability. Set allowed attempts and stop conditions using the user's budget; if budget is unknown, prepare the test without assuming paid authorization. Log every attempt, including failures and discarded outputs. Do not silently give the preferred route more curation or post-processing.

## Judge outputs at the right level

View original-speed motion and sound as well as diagnostic frames. Compare matched timestamps/events, not frame numbers at different FPS. Inspect identity, performance, contact/physics, camera/staging, prompt compliance and edit continuity separately. Blind paired viewing is preferable where practical; state who rated, what they saw and whether judgment was independent.

Automated metrics are supporting measurements. Technical fidelity benchmarks do not measure narrative meaning or prove market appeal. Use validated metric implementations only when their scope fits the assets; preserve version and parameters. Do not compute fake benchmark scores from prose reviews.

## Decide and preserve

Report raw counts, accepted useful seconds, total spend, labor, latency and known uncertainties. Tiny pilot samples support a next-step choice, not broad statistical superiority. Migrate by shot family after a successful pilot and a rollback plan. Preserve accepted outputs and their old adapter versions; do not regenerate a finished film merely because a model launched.

Deliver experiment manifest, review, decision and adapter correction where justified. Promotion to adopted requires actual local evidence, not this skill's creation.
