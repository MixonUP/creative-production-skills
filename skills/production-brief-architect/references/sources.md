Transferred principles: a concrete experience, a representative playable chain,
explicit priorities, compact state, IDs/receipts, integration ownership and real evidence.
The reports also motivate corrections: assisted progression, uncompleted gates, offline
video and unloaded hooks are not proof of a finished or fully autonomous production.

Video-side guidance comes from existing local cinema-production, film-shot-planning
and cinema-visual-development skills: scene/shot contracts, asset/state continuity,
provider-independent intent, early assembly and distinct technical/artistic acceptance.
Skill design follows the local system skill-creator instructions.

The new authoring workflow can be reviewed with synthetic brief cases without running
games, training models or generating media. Such review tests the document's decisions
and structure; it does not establish the final artistic quality, model compliance,
cost or production speed. First real productions should add dated evidence and narrow
corrections instead of broad new rules.
