# Evidence ledger
Reviewed 2026-10-05. These findings guide testing; none ranks today's art styles by beauty.

## Experimental sources
- Germine et al., 2015, *Individual Aesthetic Preferences for Faces Are Shaped Mostly by Environments, Not Genes*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26441352/ . Common agreement and stable individual preferences coexist. The reported common/individual variance split is task- and sample-specific; it is not a formula for beauty or a genetic percentage applicable to avatars. Operational implication: collect the actual user's paired preferences instead of treating popularity as personal attraction.
- McDonnell, Breidt & Bülthoff, 2012, *Render me Real?* https://www.scss.tcd.ie/rachel.mcdonnell/papers/Siggraph2012a.pdf . With controlled geometry/motion and specific rendering conditions, highly realistic and highly abstract versions could both appeal; some intermediate renderings appealed less. Motion artifacts affected evaluations. Do not infer that all stylized realism is bad. Operational implication: evaluate a coherent finished rendering and its motion, not a realism slider in isolation.

## Reviews and scope
- Kätsyri et al., 2015. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00390/full . Review of uncanny-valley evidence; perceptual mismatch is one supported explanation, with heterogeneous paradigms. Translate into checks for mismatched eyes/skin/animation, not a ban on CGI.
- de Borst & de Gelder, 2015. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00576/full . Review of perception of virtual versus human characters. Appearance, motion and multisensory cues interact. A beautiful still does not establish emotional credibility in dialogue.

## Professional practice, not scientific causal evidence
- Riot Character Art: https://www.riotgames.com/en/artedu/character-art . Shape, proportions and readable character fantasy must survive the intended game view.
- Riot Illaoi development: https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/dev-illaoi-in-project-l . Narrative identity and gameplay inform visual development across disciplines.
- SHIFT UP interview: https://blog.playstation.com/2024/04/08/stellar-blade-interview-creating-stylish-sci-fi-action-in-a-post-apocalyptic-world/ . Contrast between Eve and the ruined world is an intentional visual premise; polish alone is not the whole direction.
- Weta Alita case study: https://www.wetafx.co.nz/films/case-studies/alita . Production reference for a designed face carrying human performance. Technical ambition is not evidence of universal preference.

## Evidence hygiene
Record publication date separately from access date. Read methods/results before quantitative claims. Label secondary discovery, preprints and untested recommendations. Keep source-derived summaries concise; link original sources. Do not equate likes, sales, expensive production, anatomy regularity, CLIP similarity or an aesthetic model score with measured user attachment.
