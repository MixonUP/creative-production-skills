# Collection and provenance
A useful manifest records:
id, title, artist, source_page, source_kind, download_url, downloaded_at, relative_path, width, height, bytes, sha256, format, family, review_state, notes.
Unknown credit or AI use stays unknown. Preserve concept artist and 3D artist separately when both are credited.

Count:
- discovered pages;
- selected distinct works;
- downloaded files;
- visually triaged works;
- deeply reviewed finalists.
Do not interchange these numbers. A project containing multiple works can be one conservative catalog entry unless individual designs are explicitly identified.

Save a full image when publicly available. Public CDN size labels are not original-source proof. Measure decoded pixels, keep aspect ratio and watermark, label preview-only files. Do not claim 8K without dimension verification, nor source PSD/BLEND from a JPEG.
After a download failure, use an ordinary public page or author alternative. Do not bypass login, paywall, CAPTCHA or certificate warnings. Record unresolved failures; do not hammer guessed endpoints.

Gallery: author/family filters, readable thumbnails, full-resolution opening, original source links, favorite/export support. Local assets avoid later broken embeds. A review copy may include contact sheets but those do not replace full images.
Verify in a separate read pass: file exists, image decodes, dimensions/hash match. Test gallery rendering and a representative filter/favorite interaction. If the target disk is unreliable, preserve a verified staging copy and disclose its actual location.

For large batches, select varied families and inspect contact sheets for relevance before claiming a curated top set. Keep candidate and finalist status visible. Do not confuse an AI-generated portrait with an official game asset just because it resembles one.
