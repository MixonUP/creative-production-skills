# Build something the buyer can use

Adapted from Tilbury's digital-product; source audit: E:/Krea_tren_AI/Knowledge/06_Sources/Tilbury_Skills_Adaptation_2026-09-23.md. Read the character bible and requested audience/problem. Keep the persona's fictional biography separate from evidence, credentials and testimonials.

Choose a format around the user's intended outcome: a short guide when explanation is enough, a worksheet when the reader must decide, a template when they need an output, or a sequence when practice matters. Don't force a 30-day challenge, a bonus stack, serif layout or a $9–27 price. Research market/pricing claims when making them; price is a hypothesis until tested.

If the user wants options, present them. If they selected a product or delegated end-to-end creation, build it without mandatory idea/sample approvals. A sample unit is an internal quality anchor for consistency, not a new approval gate. Ask when a missing consequential choice really blocks the work.

Make a concrete specification: buyer, promised deliverable, scope, structure, usable exercises/artifacts, completion criterion, brand voice and output format. Claims must be supported and limitations honest even if they weaken the sales pitch. Don't represent generated advice or invented success stories as validated outcomes.

Draft enough material to fulfill the promise, with worked examples and useful navigation. Add progress/recovery guidance only when the product needs it; don't pad with generic motivational pages. Provide relevant accessibility and print/screen considerations. Avoid blanket font/glyph bans: select fonts that support the actual language and verify rendering.

Use the installed documents skill for DOCX, pdf for PDF, spreadsheets for workbooks, or presentations for decks as appropriate. Render and inspect the real artifact. Check missing/duplicated units, usable writing space, links, factual claims, page numbers, tables and final reading flow. File creation is not usability validation; report what was inspected.

Deliver the artifact, concise accurate description, optional sales copy and any unresolved checks. Creating a product does not authorize listing it, creating a storefront, payment setup, emails or publishing. Record the result in the project journal with the source paths and evidence.
