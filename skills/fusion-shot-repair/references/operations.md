Read film-postproduction when modifying a live Resolve project; preserve its timeline/frame-range rules. Consult only the relevant Fusion/finishing reference.

Example acceptance cases: a jacket logo that slips at an occlusion is not repaired merely because the starting frame matches; a dark EXR may reflect a missing view transform rather than underexposure; a missing Cryptomatte node in Free requires checking the Fuse independently, not buying Studio by default; a deliberate animated texture should not be removed by a generic denoise prescription.
